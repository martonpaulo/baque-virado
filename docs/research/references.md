# Reference recordings

The recordings each show is modelled on. The owner chose every song here (2026-11-16 and
2026-11-17). Only the owner names a reference: the songs the agent had listed from memory were
removed on 2026-11-17.

How to use this list:

- A rhythm has four references and they weigh the same: none is «the main one» and none is only a
  «similar song». What the four share is the rhythm; what only one has is that song.
- Listen to, or measure, the whole of each recording, every section of it (introduction, verse,
  chorus, breaks, solos, ending), not a thirty-second excerpt.
- Read the four against the literature and the records in this folder: what they share with what
  is written about the rhythm is what a show brings forward.
- The shows stay instrumental: where a recording has a voice, only its instrumental base counts.
- Every melody on the site is original. A reference shapes the groove, the instruments and the
  mood, never a melody copied note for note.
- The result must be striking, characteristic and something people want to listen to, not a
  demonstration ([`../product.md`](../product.md), Rhythm shows).
- A «search» link opens a YouTube search for the song; a full address is the video the owner gave.
  The agent cannot open YouTube from a cloud session ([`sound-lab.md`](sound-lab.md), Getting
  reference audio and recordings), so it has not checked which video a search returns.

## Maracatu de baque virado

No reference recording is named. The show predates this list: it follows the nações' baque as
[`pernambuco-rhythms-feeling.md`](pernambuco-rhythms-feeling.md) describes, its alfaia patterns come
from the hand table at lansdownsoftware.com, and its swing was measured on a phone recording of a
Batuk Nagô workshop (YouTube `hxW-td9bNsg`), as [`../product.md`](../product.md) records under
«Evidence on hand». The owner asked not to change this show in the 2026-11-17 round.
