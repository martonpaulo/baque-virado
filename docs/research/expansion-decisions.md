# Expansion decisions: new rhythms for Batuque

Decisions for expanding the site from one maracatu show to seven rhythm shows. Date: 2026-11-17.

## Rhythms

The project stays instrumental. No show uses a voice.

|   # | Rhythm                   | Status              |
| --: | :----------------------- | :------------------ |
|   1 | Maracatu de baque virado | Already on the site |
|   2 | Frevo                    | New                 |
|   3 | Manguebeat               | New                 |
|   4 | Coco da Xambá            | New                 |
|   5 | Brega                    | New                 |
|   6 | Bregafunk                | New                 |
|   7 | Forró pé-de-serra        | New                 |

Coco de embolada was removed. It is led by the voice, and the project is instrumental.

## General decisions

- Melodies are original. They must be strong enough that people want to listen, as in the baque
  virado show. They follow the style of each rhythm, and they do not copy existing songs.
- Forró uses the pé-de-serra trio plus the extra instruments of forró universitário bands: rabeca,
  bandolim, cavaquinho and bass.
- Each show keeps one tempo. The tempo never changes inside a show.
- Rhythms in 2/4 (frevo, coco, forró) use an 8-step wheel. One turn is one bar.
- Some sounds have no free recording: zabumba, rabeca, accordion, alto sax, distorted guitar and
  the Xambá drums. They are synthesized or built from similar sounds.
- The site name is «Batuque».
- The site calls romantic brega «Brega».
- Words on the site and in the shows are terms really used in Pernambuco, musical terms and the
  terms the academic sources use. No catchy phrases. The copy stays as clean as in the baque
  virado show.

## Feelings per show

Each show has its own feeling, and not every show is joyful. The music should make people feel
sadness, joy, fury, anger, sensuality, love, saudade or celebration. The working map:

- Frevo: euphoria, with the fury of the abafo.
- Manguebeat: anger and weight, in a minor key.
- Coco da Xambá: the trance and devotion of the terreiro.
- Brega: saudade and heartbreak, in a minor key.
- Bregafunk: sensuality and provocation.
- Forró: saudade of the sertão inside the party, in mixolydian (flat seventh).

## Coco da Xambá

- Name on the site: «Coco da Xambá». Feeling: the trance and devotion of the terreiro.
- Sources: Castro, ANPPOM 2018; a Revista Continente article on Xambá coco.
- Instruments: alfaia played on both heads, ganzá, pandeiro, palmas, agogô, abê and caixa. The low
  head plays on the beats and the second head on the off-beats.
- No melodic instrument. The show is percussion only, as in the baque virado show. The alfaia
  phrases carry the line.
- 2/4 at 132 BPM, an estimate to measure on recordings. 8-step wheel, one bar per turn, one tempo.
- Script, 12 scenes:

|   # | Scene       | Bars | Content                       |
| --: | :---------- | ---: | :---------------------------- |
|   1 | Introdução  |    4 | Alfaia roll                   |
|   2 | Alfaia      |    8 | Low head on the beats         |
|   3 | Duas peles  |    8 | Second head on the off-beats  |
|   4 | Ganzá       |    8 | Ganzá                         |
|   5 | Pandeiro    |    8 | Pandeiro                      |
|   6 | Palmas      |    8 | Palmas on the off-beats       |
|   7 | Agogô e abê |    8 | Agogô and abê                 |
|   8 | Caixa       |    8 | Caixa                         |
|   9 | Frases      |   16 | Alfaia phrases in sequence    |
|  10 | Toque       |   16 | More alfaia notes, same tempo |
|  11 | Rufo        |    4 | Final alfaia roll             |
|  12 | Final       |    8 | Cut                           |

## Visuals per rhythm

- The background animation follows the feeling of each rhythm, not the maracatu look.
- The widgets (wheel, lanes, readout panels, swing table, score, waves) are chosen per rhythm by
  what makes sense for it. The maracatu set is not a fixed list. Other rhythms may use other
  widgets or new ones.
- The first coco da Xambá build followed the maracatu layouts. Its visuals and widgets are to be
  revised.
- Backgrounds are circular and come out of the central wheel, for every rhythm.

## Colour palettes: a semiotic reading

This is a proposal, still open for discussion.

Method, after Peirce:

- Icon: the colour of the materials of the instruments.
- Index: the place and occasion where the rhythm happens.
- Symbol: the colour the culture already gave the rhythm.

Feeling sets temperature and saturation. Rhythm sets contrast: fast and accented means high
contrast, and trance or slow means close tones. The cultural symbols below come from general
knowledge. They are not researched yet and must be confirmed before the build.

- **Frevo** (euphoria, 140 BPM, brass). Icon: brass gold. Index: an Olinda street in the sun.
  Symbol: the four umbrella colours. Palette: red, yellow, green and blue on night blue, with
  brass gold, at maximum contrast. The current sketch is right.
- **Coco da Xambá** (trance, devotion, alfaia with two heads). Icon: raw leather and wood. Index:
  a beaten-earth terreiro at night, in candlelight. Symbol: Iansã, the orixá of the house (Ilê Axé
  Oyá Meguê, Santa Bárbara), in burgundy and copper. Palette: burgundy and copper on dark earth,
  with tones close together, because trance is repetition. The current sketch is too generic.
- **Manguebeat** (anger, weight, alfaia and distorted guitar). Icon: iron, rust and cable. Index:
  the green-brown mud of the mangrove and the concrete city. Symbol: the antenna in the mud and the
  red-orange crab. Palette: muddy brown-green and concrete grey, grainy, with crab orange only on
  accents, at low saturation. The current sketch lacks the mangrove green.
- **Forró pé-de-serra** (saudade inside the party; sanfona, zabumba, triângulo). Icon: the red and
  mother-of-pearl of the sanfona, leather and steel. Index: the sertão at dusk, the bonfire.
  Symbol: São João and its bandeirinhas. Palette: a dusk sky from orange to dark blue, because
  saudade is light leaving, with the bonfire at the centre and sanfona red on accents. The current
  sketch is too orange, and the incoming blue is missing.
- **Brega** (heartbreak; keyboard, guitar). Icon: stage red and gold, the glow of the
  keyboard. Index: low bar light, red neon. Symbol: the red rose. Palette: wine red and low amber
  light, blurred, warm but dark. The current sketch is too pink. Pink reads as tenderness, and
  brega is about pain.
- **Bregafunk** (sensuality, provocation; lata, 808). Icon: the metal of the tin can, the LEDs of
  the sound systems (paredões). Index: a night party in the periphery, a motorbike, a phone
  filming. Symbol: the neon purple and lime of the parties and covers. Palette: deep purple, lime
  flashes on the latas and gold on the drops, with high contrast and hard cuts. The current sketch
  is wrong: it looks like a generic club and repeats the romantic pink.
- **Instrument colours.** The wheel and lanes use the material colour of each instrument inside the
  palette of the rhythm: alfaia leather, gonguê and agogô iron grey, brass gold, sanfona red. The
  colour teaches the instrument, and the background never hides the wheel.
